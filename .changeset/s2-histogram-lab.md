Added a `Histogram` to the S2 lab that bins a numeric field and draws the count per bin on a continuous axis. It supports `binCount`, `binSize`, `binEdges`, `binAnchor` and `extent`; stacked, overlaid or dodged series; weighted bins; percent, density and cumulative values; horizontal bars; a density curve; 2D histograms with a color legend; three draw-in animations; and `ChartInspect`, `ChartPopover` and `onClick`.
