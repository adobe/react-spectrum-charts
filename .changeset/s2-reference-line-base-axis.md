`ReferenceLine` now draws on the base axis. On a `bottom` or `top` axis it is a full-height vertical line. Its label sits at the top right, moves to the top left when the right side has no room, and is hidden if neither side fits. On a `left` or `right` band, point, or time axis it is a full-width line. Base axis lines have no carets.
