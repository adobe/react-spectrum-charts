# Donut

<StageBadge />

Donuts show how categories contribute to a whole. Each data point becomes a segment sized by its
`metric` value.

<DonutPlayground />

## Data

`data` goes on `Chart` as an array of objects, one per segment. On `Donut`, `metric` names the
numeric field that sizes each segment (default `value`) and `color` names the field that
identifies it in the legend, labels, and tooltips (default `series`). Most examples on this page
use `browserData`:

<DataCode name="browserData" />

```tsx
<Chart data={browserData}>
  <Donut metric="count" color="browser" />
</Chart>
```

### Boolean data

For a single rate, like a success rate, pass exactly two points whose values sum to 1 and set
`isBoolean`. `DonutSummary` then shows the first point as a percentage instead of the total.

<DataCode name="booleanData" />

<DonutBooleanPlayground />

### Zero, empty, and loading

When every value is 0, the donut draws an empty gray ring and `DonutSummary` shows 0. When `data`
is an empty array, `Chart` shows `emptyStateText` instead. Set `loading` on `Chart` to show a
spinner while data loads.

<DataCode name="zeroData" />

<DonutStatesPlayground />

## Shape

### holeRatio

`holeRatio` is the inner radius as a fraction of the outer radius. The default is `0.85`; `0`
renders a pie chart.

<DonutHoleRatioPlayground />

### Semicircle

`variant="semicircle"` draws the top half of the circle, from 9 o'clock to 3 o'clock. Semicircles
sort segments largest-first by default. For ordered categories like survey responses, set
`sortOrder="data"` to keep the source order. Full circles always use the source order, starting
at 12 o'clock.

<DataCode name="surveyData" />

<DonutSurveyPlayground />

:::note
Semicircles don't support `SegmentLabel`.
:::

### Size

`Chart` fills the width of its container and is 300px tall by default. The donut grows to fit
the smaller of the two, so a semicircle in a wide container can be much larger than a full circle
at the same height. Use `width`, `height`, `minWidth`, and `maxWidth` on `Chart` to constrain it.
`DonutSummary` text scales with the donut.

<DonutSizePlayground />

## DonutSummary

`DonutSummary` shows a label and the sum of all segments in the center of the donut.

- `numberFormat` formats the value. It accepts `shortNumber`, `standardNumber`, or a
  [d3-format](https://d3js.org/d3-format) specifier.
- `delta` adds a change below the label, green when zero or positive and red when negative
  (`0.025` shows as `+2.5%`).
- `hideValue` shows only the label.

Numbers follow the chart's `locale`, which also applies to segment labels, `ChartInspect`, and
`ChartPopover`.

<DonutSummaryPlayground />

## SegmentLabel

`SegmentLabel` labels each segment with its name and value.

- `value` and `percent` choose what follows the name; format them with `valueFormat` and
  `percentFormat`.
- `swatch` adds the segment color before the name.
- `showValueRow` adds a smaller row below with the segment's value, and `showTotal` adds the total
  to that row, like `10,390 / 36,083`.

<SegmentLabelPlayground />

Labels sit at the middle of each segment. A label is hidden when it would overlap a label for a
larger segment or when its segment is narrower than about 17°. Hovering a segment always shows
its label and hides any labels that overlap it.

### labelKey

Labels show the `color` field by default. To show different text, such as a longer display name,
set `labelKey` to another field in the data.

<DataCode name="browserLabelData" />

<SegmentLabelKeyPlayground />

### Emphasized segments

`emphasizedItems` on `Donut` keeps the listed segments in color and grays out the rest. Add two
`SegmentLabel` children with `labelMode="emphasized"` and `labelMode="deemphasized"` to label
each group differently, or set `hideDeemphasizedLabels` to label only the emphasized segments.

<DonutEmphasisPlayground />

## Legend

Add `Legend` as a sibling of `Donut` to list the segments. The donut shrinks to make room for it.

- `isToggleable` lets users click an entry to hide or show its segment. The remaining segments fill
  the circle and `DonutSummary` updates its total.
- `highlight` highlights a segment while its entry is hovered.

<DonutLegendPlayground />

### Hiding segments

`hiddenSeries` on `Chart` hides segments from outside the chart, for example from a filter
control. The remaining segments fill the circle and `DonutSummary` totals only what's visible. To
start with segments hidden but let users toggle them, set `defaultHiddenSeries` on a toggleable
`Legend` instead.

<DonutHiddenSeriesPlayground />

## Interaction

### ChartInspect and ChartPopover

Add `ChartInspect` to show details on hover and `ChartPopover` to show details on click. Without
children, both show the segment's color, name, share of the total, and value (for Chrome,
`28.8% (10.4K)`), formatted with the chart's `locale`. Pass a function as children to render your
own content.

`rightClick` on `ChartPopover` opens it on right-click instead of click.

<DonutInteractionPlayground />

Hovering an interactive segment fades the other segments and their labels. A donut without
`ChartInspect`, `ChartPopover`, `SegmentLabel`, or a `Legend` with `highlight` doesn't respond to
the pointer.

### Highlighting

`highlightedSeries` on `Chart` highlights a segment from outside the chart, for example from a
table row. `highlightedItem` does the same by row ID and can take a list; set `idKey` on `Chart`
to the field that uniquely identifies each row. `Legend` with `highlight` highlights a segment
when its entry is hovered.

<DonutHighlightPlayground />

## Animation

The chart's `animationTypes` lists which animations play. By default, `hover` eases segments in
and out of their faded state on hover, and `drawIn` sweeps the segments in over 750ms when the
chart mounts. Labels fade in as their segment finishes; `DonutSummary` stays visible throughout.

<DonutAnimationPlayground />

Draw-in plays once when the chart mounts; re-renders, data updates, and resizing don't replay it.
Set `animationTypes={['hover']}` to turn off draw-in only, or `animations={false}` to turn off all
animation. See [Animation](./guides/animation.mdx).
